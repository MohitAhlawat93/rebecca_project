Thoughtful gestures

Never required. Always noticed.

Gifts are not expected, but Rebecca openly appreciates thoughtful gestures. Her public favourites include light-coloured flowers, wine and champagne, beautifully sourced food ingredients, lingerie, spa or experience gifts and 18K white or rose gold jewellery.

She also maintains a wishlist to remove the guesswork. If you want to surprise her with something size-specific or especially personal, asking first is usually the better move.
