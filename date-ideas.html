Date ideas & wishlist

Have a plan. Leave room for a detour.

I’m happy to help with ideas, but I have a soft spot for someone who arrives with a plan. Glamorous or low-key both work when there is some thought behind it.

Foodie is overused. Appetite is not.

Tasting menus, good wine, museums, spas, theatre, racing and the occasional terrible idea.

Things I actually enjoy

A date should give us something to talk about.

Feed me well and I’m already in a good mood: tasting menus, sushi, steak, seafood, wine pairings and hidden bars are easy wins. I’m equally happy at a museum, theatre performance, beach club, couples spa, movie, arcade, go-kart track or somewhere neither of us has tried before.

I also love cooking classes, wine and sommelier tastings, fitness, shopping dates and the kind of afternoon that accidentally becomes dinner.

Not spoiled, just well-loved

Thoughtful beats obligatory.

Gifts are never required, but I do appreciate people who notice details. Flowers, beautiful food, lingerie, wine, champagne, spa experiences and jewellery are all firmly within my interests.

Current favourites include light-coloured roses, peonies, hydrangeas and orchids; Krug and other good bottles; premium food ingredients; and 18K white or rose gold jewellery. I also keep a wishlist when guessing is not your preferred sport.

Pick the time first.

Once the date is confirmed, we can decide whether we are dressing up, disappearing somewhere quiet, or doing something slightly ridiculous together.
