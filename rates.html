Useful details

Before you choose.

Rates are fixed and not subject to negotiation. A deposit is required to confirm every date.
Extensions

In-date extensions are SGD 800 per additional hour, subject to Rebecca’s availability.

Long private dates

For dates of 4 hours or longer where you prefer not to venture out, add a flat SGD 500 and include room service.

Hosting in Singapore

Hosting starts from SGD 400 with a 1.5-hour minimum. Rebecca hosts only occasionally in Singapore, with exact opportunities shared selectively.

Couples

There is a 2-hour minimum and an additional SGD 500 consideration.

Planning call

A screened 20-minute call can be arranged for SGD 250 if you would like to plan before confirming a date.
