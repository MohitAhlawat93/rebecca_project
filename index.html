01 The introduction

Equal parts arm candy and brain food.

My mind has never been especially linear, and neither are my favourite dates. Give me good food, an interesting person and enough time, and conversation tends to wander somewhere much better than either of us planned.

I’ve lived, studied and worked across three continents, visited countries, and somehow remain convinced there is always another restaurant, city, book, bottle or wonderfully niche rabbit hole worth exploring.

I like beautiful things, but ease matters more than spectacle. Freedom is the real luxury: time to linger, disappear for a while, and enjoy each other without watching the clock.
