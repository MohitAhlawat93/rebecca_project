The introduction

More than the photograph.

Warm, witty, adventurous and unusually curious. Rebecca values authenticity over performance, chemistry over convention, and the kind of dates that become private jokes rather than calendar entries.

52 countries · two languages · one very curious mind

The best version of Rebecca’s world is less résumé, more atmosphere.

Her story

Equal parts arm candy and brain food.

Well-educated, well-travelled and occasionally well-behaved, Rebecca has lived, studied and worked across three continents and visited 52 countries. Her favourite conversations rarely stay on one subject for long: food, history, behavioural psychology, F1, wine, travel, books and whatever wonderfully niche obsession has appeared that week.

Established since 2015, she gravitates toward unhurried companionship, genuine connection and relationships that can grow over time. Luxury, in her view, is less about spectacle than ease: the freedom to linger, disappear for a while and enjoy someone’s company without rushing the clock.

Her public persona is polished, but not precious. She is equally at home around a tasting menu, a spontaneous adventure, a quiet morning, a good bottle or a truly terrible joke.

The short interview

A little personality before the hello.

What makes a good first impression?

Efficiency, good manners, reliability and a thoughtful introduction. If you are new, read the etiquette and contact guidance first; Rebecca is very comfortable meeting first-timers who can follow instructions.

What does an ideal date feel like?

Natural, attentive and unhurried. Good food, easy chemistry, a little spontaneity and enough time to forget you are looking at the clock.

What does Rebecca talk about?

Reading, fitness, travel, culture, history, cooking, wine, anthropology, economics, F1 and cars—plus whatever tangent the evening creates.

What are the non-negotiables?

Mutual discretion, respect, good hygiene, clear communication and boundaries that are respected the first time they are expressed.
