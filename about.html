The introduction

Professionally flirtatious. Terminally curious.

I’m Singaporean, well-travelled, relentlessly curious and much more interested in authenticity than performance. If you enjoy people with opinions, appetites and stories, we’ll probably have plenty to talk about.

52 countries · two languages · still curious

I collect beautiful experiences, adventures and secrets like a never-ending scrapbook.

A little context

Beautiful things. Interesting people. No beige conversation.

I’ve lived, studied and worked across three continents and visited 52 countries so far. Travel has made me adaptable, nosy in the best possible way, and very good at finding something interesting to ask you about.

My favourite conversations wander between food, books, wine, culture, history, psychology, economics, F1 and whatever unexpected tangent appears over dinner. I read several books at once and am spectacularly indecisive when a menu gives me too many good options.

I’m drawn to longer, unhurried dates and connections that have a chance to become familiar over time. A beautiful hotel is lovely; feeling completely at ease with someone is better.

I can be polished when the occasion calls for it, but I’m not especially interested in pretending to be mysterious for sport. Expect warmth, candour, terrible jokes and a woman who is very present when she is with you.

A few questions

Useful information, with personality.

How do I make a good first impression?

Be efficient, reliable and capable of reading instructions. Thoughtful gestures are lovely, but making plans easy is genuinely attractive.

What does an ideal date feel like?

Natural enough that we forget we are trying to impress each other. Good food helps. So do private jokes, a little spontaneity and enough time to wonder why we didn’t meet sooner.

What makes you happy?

Balance: enough security to make choices freely, and enough freedom to follow my curiosity wherever it goes.

Pet peeves?

Bad manners, bad wine and wilful intellectual laziness. Rate negotiation deserves an honourable mention.

Are you really this opinionated in person?

Probably worse.

Nothing risqué, nothing gained.
