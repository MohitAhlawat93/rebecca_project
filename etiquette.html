Rules of engagement

Common sense. Good manners. Easy.

If you are interested in meeting me, all I ask is that you read the practical rules first. They protect both of us and make everything after that considerably more relaxed.

Privacy is mutual

Discretion, reliability and boundaries are not optional extras.

Screening & privacy

I need to know who I’m meeting.

Screening is required. Accepted routes include LinkedIn, ID and employment verification, among other options. If I need more information, I will ask; if I am not comfortable, I may decline.

Screening information is for my eyes only and is deleted after verification. If you prefer, sensitive material can be sent as a disappearing message through my verified WhatsApp or Telegram.

Please do not send ID, employer documents or other sensitive screening material to the website concierge.

Deposits

A date is confirmed when the deposit is in.

Singapore20–25% minimum
Touring40% minimum
Fly me to you50% + travel expenses

Cancellations & communication

Life happens. Tell me early.

48+ hours’ notice

A deposit can usually be transferred to a future date after non-refundable costs are deducted.

Touring / hosting / gift-card deposits

These are non-refundable because costs may already have been incurred.

Last-minute cancellation

A last-minute cancellation carries a 100% cancellation fee. If you cut a confirmed date short, the originally agreed rate still applies.

If I have to cancel

If I cancel for ordinary reasons, your deposit is returned in full. Unsafe, pushy or disrespectful behaviour is a separate matter and ends the booking immediately.

When should I contact you?

When you are ready to complete screening and send the required deposit within 24 hours after details are agreed.

Manners maketh man

A few non-negotiables.

My rates are not negotiated. A boundary should never need to be stated twice. Private conversations, contact information and anything shared in confidence stay between us.

I do not meet “off the clock.” I do not publicly show my face and I do not send extra private selfies on request. Outfit requests are welcome; micromanagement is not.

If we ever cross paths socially, treat me exactly as I would treat you: like a stranger who happened to make eye contact.

Still straightforward?

Excellent. Read the rates, choose a date and duration, then send me a complete introduction.
