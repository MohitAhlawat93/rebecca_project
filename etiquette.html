Rules of engagement

Clear expectations. Easier dates.

Most of Rebecca’s etiquette is simply good manners made explicit: protect each other’s privacy, communicate clearly, respect boundaries, and only enquire when you are genuinely ready to move forward.

Discretion is part of the experience

Clear boundaries create the ease everyone actually came for.

Before confirmation

Screening & privacy.

What screening is required?

Screening is required. Rebecca publicly lists options including LinkedIn, ID and employment verification, and may ask for additional information at her discretion.

Do not upload ID documents to this website or send them to the concierge. Screening material should be sent privately through Rebecca’s verified contact channels.

How is screening information handled?

Rebecca’s published policy says screening information is for her eyes only and deleted after verification. Mutual discretion remains a priority before, during and after a meeting.

When should I reach out?

Reach out when you are ready to provide the required screening and, once details are agreed, send the deposit within the expected timeframe.

Deposits

A date becomes real when it is confirmed.

Singapore20–25% minimum
Touring40% minimum
Fly me to you50% + travel expenses
Deposits are required for all confirmed dates, including repeat meetings. Payment methods are discussed privately.

Cancellations

Life happens. Communication matters.

48+ hours’ notice

A deposit may be transferable to a future date within a reasonable timeframe after deducting non-refundable costs.

Touring, hosting and gift-card deposits

Rebecca’s published policy treats these as non-refundable because costs may already have been incurred.

Last-minute cancellation

Last-minute cancellations can carry the full cancellation fee. A date that is cut short by the guest still carries the originally agreed consideration.

If Rebecca has to cancel

Her published policy says the deposit is returned in full if she has to cancel.

During & after

Manners maketh the meeting.

Rates are not negotiated. Boundaries are respected immediately. Private conversations, personal contact information and anything shared in confidence remain between the two of you.

Rebecca does not publicly show her face and asks visitors not to request additional private selfies. Outfit requests are welcome when they stay on the right side of micromanagement.

If you ever cross paths socially, discretion is simple: treat each other as strangers who happen to have made eye contact.

Read once. Enjoy the date.
