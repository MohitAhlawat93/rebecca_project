L’introduction

Professionnellement charmeuse. Inlassablement curieuse.

Singapourienne, très voyageuse et curieuse, je m’intéresse bien plus à l’authenticité qu’à la performance. Si vous aimez les gens qui ont des opinions, de l’appétit et des histoires, nous aurons de quoi parler.

countries · two languages · still curious

I collect beautiful experiences, adventures and secrets like a never-ending scrapbook.

A little context

Beautiful things. Interesting people. No beige conversation.

I’ve lived, studied and worked across three continents and visited countries so far. Travel has made me adaptable, nosy in the best possible way, and very good at finding something interesting to ask you about.

My favourite conversations wander between food, books, wine, culture, history, psychology, economics, F1 and whatever unexpected tangent appears over dinner. I read several books at once and am spectacularly indecisive when a menu gives me too many good options.

I’m drawn to longer, unhurried dates and connections that have a chance to become familiar over time. A beautiful hotel is lovely; feeling completely at ease with someone is better.

I can be polished when the occasion calls for it, but I’m not especially interested in pretending to be mysterious for sport. Expect warmth, candour, terrible jokes and a woman who is very present when she is with you.
